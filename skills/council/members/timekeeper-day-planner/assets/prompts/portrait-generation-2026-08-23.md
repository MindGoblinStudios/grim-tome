# Timekeeper Portrait Prompt

Model: MidJourney, landscape source center-cropped to a 1024x1024 `portrait.png`; icons are resized from the full frame.

Prompt (reconstructed): a wooden and brass hourglass with glowing gold sand standing in a vast dark stone hall beneath a giant blue clock face, a hooded figure in a forest-green cloak kneeling behind it, towering black pillars, cold teal light, warm candlelit glow from the sand, painterly dark fantasy.

Note: Timekeeper is the hourglass. The green-hooded figure is a carrier, not Timekeeper.
