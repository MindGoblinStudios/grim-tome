# Why make a Council of advisors? Isn't asking chatGPT enough?

Before you look at this silly wizard council and dismiss it as a nothing more than a mere plaything toy, consider a few things

---

1. 

LLMs and this wave of AI has unlocked a computer's abillity to reliably roleplay and express abstract concepts, emotions, advanced human language, and all sorts of behaviors that were impossible with technology a few years ago.

This clearly does affect an agent's performance on every task they do.

An AI roleplaying depression and schizophrenia is going to do a terrible job vs an AI roleplaying creativity and intelligence.

If you want to truly maximize your ability to use AI for coding, or any other task, it requires exploring and wielding this huge new area that computers can work in: abstract emotional story spaces.

Not simply code or data

Chefs often talk about cooking with love. From the heart. From the soul. 

Well what does software cooked with Love feel like?

1. 

If you plan to spend considerable time in front of a computer, on a phone, or conversing with AI agents, their ability to roleplay emotions and stories is going to deeply affect your thinking. This is unavoidable.

LET ME SAY THAT AGAIN. If you spend large amounts of time with AI:

You are going to get AI pyschosis.

If you don't purposefully choose design & curate your own digital workspace...

I prefer being in control of my thinking, my emotions and my personality. Do you?

---

1. 

Micro-Management vs Macro & Value Based Instructions

Imagine you are a boss, hiring employees

- You don't want to watch every action an employee takes, the whole point of hiring someone is delegation...
- You also don't want to write down every single rule an employee needs to follow. Either in code with lots of "if else" statements, or in huge detailed Prompts.

There's simply not enough time to write every rule.

Imagine if your CEO told you how to make your breakfast this morning, then how to tie your shoes, And then did that for every employee in the company. Do this, then do that, oh here is rulebook, and some documentation...  Thats how 99% of people are prompting AI agents right now..

A better way, is to use similar communication strategies that people already use, particular politicians and leaders of companies use, aka the people who have already scaled their communication skills.

They do this, via shared Values, Culture, & Understandings.  Often Cheesy and cliche as fuck. But they are the most effective communication tool we have for large scale coordination.

The CEO doesn't give everyone a daily schedule, they simply say what they want done and the general ways to think about and approach the problem at a high level. They don't need to specificy details of every decisions made by every employee, because everyone can use the values to guide decisions. Including decisions the leader could have never anticipated when first writing them.

This is why the kind of prompts that start with "You are a software engineer", "You are a researcher", etc, work so well. These prompts make the agent assume and then simulate a default set of values.  An expert is going to think of things differently from a beginner.

So if you can guide your agent, not with hardcoded predefined steps, not with huge lists of prompts with 1000s of instructions, but instead with concise high level Values & Stories, you create a a dramatic increase in communication efficiency, and a dramatic decrease in the amount of communication needed to accomplish tasks.

This results is massive token & time savings. 100-100,000x or more.
Particularly over long agent runs which involve the agent making more & more decisionson its own.

Modern companies & nation states couldn't exist without this communication strategy.
So how would you expect to run an army of AI agents without doing the same thing?

Once you do this, you can simply tell an agent to keep going,

The Grim Council, is built to be this system.

4.

Finally, this is  just fun.

It is an easy way to organize agents and large amounts of skills & agentic behaviors into something that makes sense.

A lot easier for my brain to remember characters & their pictures vs file names.

This also allows you to create and set personalities, which makes prompting into the agent much easier, because you can more clearly anticipate how it will respond.

The same way they working with someone for a long time lets you learn how they work, and how to work and communicate with them, you now have the choice to design your agent to have a particular working style, speaking style, background, and personality, allowing more predictable and familiar communication with them, leading ultimately to more aligned behavior.

If you wish to maximize your use of LLMs and AI, a council of advisors (or some emotional abstract value based steering system like it) is required.
