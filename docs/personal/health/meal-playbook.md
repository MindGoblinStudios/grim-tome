## Planning Defaults

- Check the active meal plan and food inventory before buying more.
- Give `Use now` food a real meal slot.
- Keep one home-cooked, one restaurant, and one nearly effortless fallback available.
- Use the restaurant directory for known orders instead of researching from scratch.
- Add recipes to the recipe index, then improve them with dated cooking notes.
- Prefer a workable week over a comprehensive diet system.
